Before we begin

This study includes a short visual activity and a Google Form. You can complete both in one visit. Taking part is voluntary.

Study information & privacy

The task may cause mild fatigue. Stop if uncomfortable. There is no flashing or sound. You can stop before submitting without penalty. After submission, responses cannot be withdrawn. Do not collect data while driving or crossing a road.

We store coded reaction times, timing-quality checks, consent and basic device/browser details in a private Google Sheet controlled by the research team. Google Forms store the survey responses in that Sheet too. We do not ask for your name, email, diagnosis, camera or microphone access. Your random code links the game and survey records. Avoid identifying details in optional answers.

Google and the hosting provider process requests under their own policies. The research team is Group 57. You may leave before submitting. Your activity responses are sent when you choose Save activity and continue to Form after the third stage. The Google Form submits the questionnaire separately. Submitted responses cannot be withdrawn. Local pending records are kept only to retry a failed save. A code is not a guarantee of anonymity.
